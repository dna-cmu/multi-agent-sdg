# Extraction re-run without the 12,000-character cut: plan fixed before any API call (26 Nov 2026)

Status: exploratory robustness check requested by DNA after the authors' extraction audit (SI S16). Results are preliminary and are reported to DNA first; whether and how they enter the paper is decided afterwards. Nothing in the frozen Version 4 instrument (scoring prompt, retrieval, aggregation, thresholds) is changed, and no existing output file is modified. This plan is written after the audit result is known, and any report of it must say so.

## What is run
1. Extraction, all 30 reports. Same model alias (gpt-4o), temperature (0.1), JSON mode, system message and extraction prompt as agents/planner.py. The only change: text is read in full. The stored chunk file of each report (data/outputs/main/chunks) is read in order; every chunk gets the section label that the frozen retrieval gives it (utils.retrieval.resolve_chunk_section on the chunk's page); consecutive chunks with the same label are joined into windows of at most 12,000 characters, cut at chunk boundaries (a single longer chunk is cut at 12,000). Each window is one call. The section labels of the original run cannot be rebuilt exactly because the chunk list of the original parse is not stored; this is a known difference.
2. New versus already extracted. A re-extracted claim is "already extracted" if, within the same report, at least 80% of its words (lower case, punctuation removed, words of one or two letters ignored) occur in one original claim or at least 80% of an original claim's words occur in it. Otherwise it is "newly captured". Exact duplicates within the re-run are counted once.
3. Sample. 1,000 newly captured claims drawn at random without replacement from all 30 reports (seed 20261126; each claim has the same chance). In addition, all newly captured claims of two reports chosen by DNA before the run because they rank their goals explicitly: PTT Global Chemical and Siam Cement Group.
4. Mapping and scoring of those claims with the unchanged Mapper and the frozen Version 4 scorer (score_v4.score_items; three runs, median; fewer than three valid runs → rescored in full up to three attempts, as in the corpus run). Evidence passages are retrieved with the frozen retrieval function from the original pool of each report (original claims and chunks); embeddings are cached under a new key so that existing caches are not touched.
5. Budget: hard ceiling US$150 in total (DNA); extraction is run first and its actual token use is checked before any mapping or scoring.

## What is reported
A. Coverage and recall of the re-run: claims per report before and after; share already extracted; recall against sentences the two authors judged to be claims in the pair audit (resolved judgements: 61 claims, 24 non-claims, 5 unresolved) and against Auditor 1's 30 listed claims on 12 pages. A sentence counts as captured if a re-extracted claim of the same report shares at least 60% of the words of the shorter of the two texts. The share of the 24 non-claims that are extracted is reported as a check on precision.
B. Newly captured against original claims (mapped claims only, as in the paper): mapping rate; SMTE tier shares; means of S, M, T, E and C; claim-type mix; mean SMTE by claim type; environmental (goals 6, 7, 12, 13, 14, 15) against social (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 10, 16) goal means. Intervals: percentile bootstrap, 2,000 replicates, claims resampled within report.
C. Combined estimate: what the corpus figures would be if all newly captured claims had been scored, weighting the sample up to the number of newly captured claims in each report.
D. The two reports scored in full: firm–goal cells under Rule A and Rule B (PRESPEC-firm-goal-rules-10Nov2026.md), with the authors' resolved declaration matrix, before and after adding the newly captured claims. Two reports are an illustration, not a test of the corpus result.

## Reading fixed in advance
"The patterns hold" means all of: (i) each SMTE tier share in the combined estimate is within 5 percentage points of the published share (37.5 / 53.1 / 9.4); (ii) Measurability and Temporality are still the two lowest dimension means among newly captured claims; (iii) the ordering of claim types by mean SMTE among newly captured claims has a Spearman correlation of at least 0.8 with the published ordering (types with at least 30 claims); (iv) the environmental minus social difference has the same sign. Any criterion not met is reported as not met. No criterion is changed after the results are seen.

## Addendum, 26 Nov 2026 (written after parts A to D were seen, before the runs below)
The first results showed that only 62% of the original claims were found again. That figure mixes run-to-run variation with the change in how the text is windowed. Four further checks, agreed with DNA; ceiling unchanged (US$150 in total).
E. Repeatability of extraction. The re-run extraction is repeated once with identical settings and windows (pass 2). Reported: claims in pass 1 and pass 2; share of pass-1 claims found in pass 2 and the reverse (same 80% word rule as part 2); size of the union; recall of each pass and of the union against the pair-audit claims and Auditor 1's listing (same 60% rule as part A).
F. Are unstable claims different? Among the 6,320 original scored claims, those found again in the re-run (pass 1) are compared with those not found again on the existing Version 4 scores: SMTE tier shares, dimension means, claim-type mix. No new scoring.
G. Repeatability of mapping. 300 original claims drawn at random (seed 20261126) from all 6,936 are mapped again with the unchanged Mapper. Reported: share mapped both times; share with an identical set of goals; share with at least one goal in common; mean number of goals each time; Cohen's κ over claim × goal cells.
H. Environmental against social goals among newly captured claims, by claim type and with the direct standardisation by claim type used in the paper (weights: the original corpus claim-type mix). Descriptive.
These checks are post hoc. E to G have no pass/fail criterion; they are reported as measured.
