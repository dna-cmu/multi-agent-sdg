# Firm–goal aggregation rules — pre-specification (approved by DNA, 10 Nov 2026)

*Written and approved on 10 Nov 2026, before the 30-report corpus is rescored with frozen V4. Committed and pushed to GitHub before the corpus run, so that the rules are fixed before any V4 corpus score exists (Reviewer 3, point 5). No V4 corpus output existed at this date (data/outputs/main/full_v4/ empty).*

DNA's decision (10 Nov 2026): report two rules side by side for comparison; neither is tuned after seeing the scores.

**Unit.** A firm–goal cell = one firm × one SDG (30 × 17 = 510 cells). A claim belongs to every goal it is mapped to. Cells with fewer than 3 mapped claims are reported as "too few claims" (robustness: 5).
**Composite.** The headline composite chosen by the pre-registered rule in FREEZE-V4-30Oct2026.md (SMTE unless raters' α for C ≥ 0.667); the other composite is reported alongside.

| Rule | A cell counts as substantiated when | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| A. Mean rule (the rule in the submitted paper, at the substantiated threshold) | the mean composite of its claims is ≥ 2.0 | Is the firm's reporting on this goal substantiated on average? |
| B. Count rule | it contains at least 1 claim with composite ≥ 2.0 (robustness: at least 3) | Does the report contain any substantiated claim supporting this goal? |

For every cell, both rules are reported, together with: number of claims; number and share of substantiated claims; mean S, M, T, E (dimension profile). Declared cells (the firm names the goal as a priority) and undeclared cells are tabulated separately under each rule.

**Wording fixed in advance (from Reviewer 3).** Under Rule A the result is stated as "no / n declared firm–goal cells meet the specified mean-score threshold", never as "the text substantiates engagement with almost no goal".
